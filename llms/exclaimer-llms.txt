# Exclaimer

> Exclaimer is a cloud-based email signature management platform that helps organizations centrally manage and deploy professional, on-brand signatures across Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and Exchange. It eliminates inconsistency, ensures legal and brand compliance, and enables marketing campaigns directly from email signatures.

## Core Use-Cases

- [Manage email signatures](https://exclaimer.com/solutions/automate-email-signatures/): Automatically create and deploy professional signatures company-wide, eliminating the need for manual updates by end-users.
- [Guarantee email compliance](https://exclaimer.com/solutions/achieve-email-compliance/): Centrally apply legal disclaimers and confidentiality notices to ensure every outbound email meets regulatory and industry standards.
- [Ensure brand consistency](https://exclaimer.com/solutions/enhance-brand-consistency/): Maintain a consistent corporate identity by governing logos, fonts, and layouts across all employee emails and video call backgrounds.
- [Run marketing campaigns](https://exclaimer.com/solutions/generate-demand/): Use email signatures as a marketing channel by adding clickable banners, tracking engagement with built-in analytics, and targeting campaigns by department, recipient, or even CRM lists.

## Core Features

- [Email Signature Manager](https://exclaimer.com/product/email-signature-management/): Core product for centrally designing and deploying professional email signatures across all employees, automatically applied to every outgoing email.
- [Disclaimers](https://exclaimer.com/product/disclaimers/): Automatically append legal disclaimers, confidentiality notices, or compliance statements to outbound emails.
- [Cloud Deployment](https://exclaimer.com/product/cloud-deployment/): Server-side signature deployment for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, ensuring consistency across all devices and email clients.
- [Device Deployment](https://exclaimer.com/product/device-deployment/): Client-side deployment that renders signatures directly within Outlook so employees see their signature while composing emails.
- [Directory Synchronization](https://exclaimer.com/product/directory-synchronization/): Syncs employee data from Azure AD, Google Directory, Okta, or HRIS platforms to keep signatures up to date automatically.
- [Brand Kits](https://exclaimer.com/product/branding/): Governs brand identity across all digital communications, ensuring signature designs adhere to brand guidelines across teams, departments, and brands.
- [Meeting Branding](https://exclaimer.com/product/meeting-branding/): Create and centrally deploy professional, on-brand video call backgrounds and personalized nametags across Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and more.
